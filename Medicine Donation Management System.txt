 Medicine Donation Management System
reduce the wastage of unused medicines by connecting medicine donors, such as pharmacies, with verified NGOs and healthcare organizations that need them.



Recipient workflow
Recipient is the person who wants to request an available medicine.
1. Recipient opens the website.
2. Registers as a Recipient.
3. Logs in.
4. Gets redirected to the Recipient Dashboard.
5. Opens Browse Medicines.
6. Views approved medicine listings.
7. Searches for a particular medicine or filters by category.
8. Opens medicine details.
9. Submits a request with the required quantity.
10. The request receives Pending status.
11. Admin reviews the request.
12. Recipient can track whether the request is approved, rejected or fulfilled
